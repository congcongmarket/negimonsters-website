NEGI MONSTERS is a two-person studio based in Shanghai, creating original soft-vinyl characters and the colorful worlds they inhabit. Each figure begins with a small story of wonder, courage, and impossible dreams.

From sculpting and casting to the final layers of paint, we work in small batches so every monster retains a little human warmth.
